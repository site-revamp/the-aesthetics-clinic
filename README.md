# README

## Website Overview

The Aesthetics Clinic is a Dubai-based medical aesthetics and dermatology clinic located at The Pavilion, Al Furjan. It provides non-surgical cosmetic treatments, dermatological care, laser procedures, skin rejuvenation, and hair restoration services.

The redesigned homepage focuses on communicating the clinic’s medical aesthetics expertise, making its treatment range easier to explore, and building patient confidence through clearer clinical information and consultation pathways.

## Main Improvements

### 1. Clearer Homepage Positioning and Hero Messaging
- Replaced the less specific opening message with clearer communication of the clinic’s aesthetic and dermatological services.
- Strengthened the supporting message to explain the clinic’s treatment focus and patient-centred approach.
- Made the consultation call to action more prominent.

**Issue addressed:** Visitors can understand the clinic’s purpose and services more quickly, with a clearer next step.

### 2. Improved Treatment Visibility and Discovery
- Brought key treatments into the homepage, including Botox, dermal fillers, HydraFacial, laser treatments, skin boosters, and hair restoration.
- Organised treatment information into clearer categories to support easier browsing.
- Improved access to relevant treatment information without requiring visitors to start on the separate Services page.

**Issue addressed:** The clinic’s broad treatment range is easier to discover and understand.

### 3. Stronger Clinical Trust and Credibility
- Gave greater visibility to the clinic’s medical team and doctors’ professional roles.
- Improved the presentation of clinical expertise and treatment-related information.
- Made trust-building content more prominent to support informed consultation decisions.

**Issue addressed:** Visitors have clearer access to information about the professionals providing care, helping address the limited clinical credibility presented on the original homepage.

## Design Improvements

- **Visual presentation:** Established a more polished healthcare and aesthetics-focused appearance.
- **Layout:** Created a clearer content hierarchy across the hero, treatment sections, and medical team content.
- **User experience:** Improved treatment discovery and made consultation actions easier to identify.
- **Consistency:** Applied a more consistent approach to section structure, typography, spacing, and content presentation.
- **Overall appearance:** Shifted the homepage towards a more organised, professional presentation aligned with the clinic’s medical aesthetics positioning.

## Project Focus

Improve homepage clarity, treatment discovery, clinical credibility, and consultation engagement while retaining the clinic’s focus on personalised aesthetic and dermatological care.
